SIH26056 India has no real-time airfare inflation index. Faresight builds one.

DGCA monthly averages arrive 2 months late. Faresight scrapes airline + OTA sites every day across 6 representative routes × 5 booking windows, removes outliers, and publishes a Base-100 index weighted by DGCA traffic. 100 = price in the base week. 104.2 = 4.2% higher.

1. ObserveScrape IndiGo, Air India, Akasa, SpiceJet, MMT, ixigo, Goibibo daily
2. CleanDe-duplicate, flag outliers with MAD, keep median fare per cell
3. IndexPrice relative × DGCA weight → daily / weekly / monthly APIx
APIx(t) = Σ [ wr × (Pr(t) / Pr(0)) ] × 100  ·  r = route

Windows: T+1 = tomorrow, T+7 = in 1 week, T+15 = 2 weeks, T+30 = 1 month, T+45 = 45 days. Advance purchase matters — same flight is cheaper 30 days out.
